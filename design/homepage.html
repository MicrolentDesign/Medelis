Build notes — not part of the page

Design v4. Palette repitched to the supplied blues and the imagery replaced.

Palette — and one constraint worth knowing

  • #0D47A1 carries everything that is text or action. 8.63:1 on white, 8.05:1 on the canvas. Buttons, links, headings, pill text, composition strips.
  • #2196F3 cannot carry text. It measures 3.12:1 on white and 2.92:1 on the canvas, so it fails AA for text and fails 1.4.11 as a state indicator on the canvas. It is used as a brightness colour only — the hero and band glows, the pill dot, the nav underline on the white nav, the newsletter button, icon accents.
  • Primary buttons brighten to #1976D2 on hover (4.60:1 with white text, still AA). That gives the palette its lift on interaction without breaking contrast.
  • Neutrals are blue-biased rather than grey, so they belong to the palette instead of sitting next to it. Body is #4A5B75 at 6.44:1 on the canvas.
  • Indigo and teal are gone entirely. Green has now been removed twice; this palette settles it.

Imagery

  • The operating-theatre hero is gone. The whole v3 set was sterilization-era. Replaced with a dispensary, stock shelving, a retail pharmacy and two unbranded pack shots.
  • The grade was the other half of "dull". v3 desaturated to 14%; this is ~85% per §8, with lifted midtones. Pack shots sit near natural because they have to read as real product.
  • Two candidates were rejected for third-party branding — one with "SANDOZ" and drug names legible, one a retail shelf of Advil and Panadol. One rejected for green capsules on a lilac ground. All recorded in design/img/MANIFEST.json.
  • The warm orange of the blister pack is the only non-blue thing on the page, and it is doing real work — a blue-on-blue palette goes monotone without it.

Still open

  • The logo still cannot be placed. The supplied PNG has the pale-blue plate and purple keyline baked in, and that purple now clashes with the blue system. Nav and footer use a type lockup. Vector still needed.
  • Product data, WhatsApp number, year established, states served, product counts.
  • Design system §3 still documents the indigo/teal palette — it needs amending to match this build.
