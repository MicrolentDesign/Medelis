Build notes — not part of the page

Design v3, rebuilt against 02-design-system.md v2.0 and 04-homepage-plan.md v2.0. The business pivoted from sterilization services to a pharmaceutical catalogue, so v2 of this page is void, not amended.

The system inverted

  • Tinted canvas, white cards. The page ground is #EDF1F8, never white. This is §3.1's defining relationship and everything else follows it.
  • Shadows replace hairlines. No card has a border. Blue-tinted elevation only.
  • Buttons are pills with a circular ↗ badge. Radius up to 16–28px.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans, headings at 700, body at 400 in slate-600. Mono restricted to product codes and stat captions.
  • Pill badges replace mono eyebrows. The only uppercase on the page.
  • Teal is back as the accent, per §3.3 — see the open question opposite.

Signature element

  • The composition strip replaces the dead cycle strip. Sans on indigo-50, two-line clamp, identical everywhere a product appears. §4.3 forbids mono here and the long combination on card three shows why.

§3.8 corrections applied

  • Muted text is slate-600, not slate-500. As specified it measures 3.85:1 on the tinted canvas and fails AA — and it is what packing lines, stat captions and product counts are all set in.
  • The "New launch" pill uses orange-900 (7.52:1) rather than orange-700 on orange-100 (4.13:1, fails at 12px). orange-900 restored to the ramp.
  • §3.7's published ratios were re-measured; seven of nine were off, though every verdict held. Corrected in the doc.

Deliberate stress cases

  • Three product cards, not six, and an incomplete row left un-stretched with no centred orphan — §10.
  • Card three carries a 66-character composition and a three-line brand name, the §7.3 survival test.
  • Pack shots are absent, so the neutral placeholder state is what renders.

Open

  • Green. You asked for it out on 5 Aug; §3.3 reinstates teal as the accent on the strength of the Medvita reference. Both cannot hold. It is a token change now and a repaint across 28 routes later.
  • Product data, WhatsApp number, year established, states served, product counts.
  • Nav label: "Products" vs "Medicines" — client's call.
